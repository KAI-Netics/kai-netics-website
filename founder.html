Executive Biography

Cybersecurity Recovery. Digital Transformation. Mission Accountability.

Kevin Alexander Smith is the Founder and CEO of KAI-Netics. Over more than three decades, he has led high-performing technical teams and complex, multi-million-dollar technology programs in commercial, government, healthcare, financial-services, education, telecommunications, and energy environments.

At Microsoft, he managed global cybersecurity infrastructure initiatives supporting the eradication of sophisticated adversaries, restoration of command and control in compromised environments, infrastructure modernization, and digital transformation programs across the Americas, APAC, and EMEA.

He brings that same discipline to autonomous operations: define the mission, govern risk, assemble the right technical team, integrate the data and systems, and stand behind the result.

Cloud and platform experience: Azure and AWS. Network and infrastructure credentials: CCNA, CWNA, and MCSE.
