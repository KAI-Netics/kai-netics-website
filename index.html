The TAO Mission Lifecycle

Assess. Plan. Integrate. Operate. Assure.

A governed five-stage approach that reduces uncertainty while preserving speed.

1

Assess

Clarify mission needs, assets, constraints, airspace, data requirements, and outcomes.

2

Plan

Design the solution, scope, safety approach, team, schedule, and compliance path.

3

Integrate

Assemble pilots, platforms, sensors, software, data workflows, and client systems.

4

Operate

Execute with disciplined communications, field quality control, and secure data handling.

5

Assure

Review results, deliver findings, document controls, and recommend continuous improvement.
